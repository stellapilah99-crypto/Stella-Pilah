Your story

Sera facility was born from a simple belief.

Quality healthcare should be close,compassionate and affordable for everyone.

We started as a small team of doctors,nurses and physiotherapists who saw a gap.

Many people were travelling for specialised care for mother and child, for rehabilitation after a stoke/injury and for honest counselling.

So, we decided to bring it under one roof.
